# About this site

This site is a readable view of the [Oregon K-12 Funding Research archive](https://github.com/pietvanzoen/fund-oregon-schools),
a collection of primary documents and cited analysis on Oregon K-12 funding: how much the state spends on public
schools, how that money is shared, and what it buys, focused on the 2027-29 budget.

## What this archive is for

- Put the facts on Oregon K-12 funding for the 2027-29 budget in one place, each one traceable to the original
  document.
- Keep a dated, word-for-word record of what officials have promised and said.
- Make it easy for anyone (parents, reporters, legislative staff) to check a number before repeating it.

It's a research archive, not a campaign: it doesn't advocate for anything or offer talking points. Campaigns can use it to
check facts and build their own materials.

## How to use this research

- **Repeating a fact:** click its citation and quote the original document, not this site.
- **Our own work:** figures marked <span class="cite cite-der">🧮 our sum</span> or
  <span class="cite cite-der">🧮 our estimate</span>, and anything marked **Analysis**, are this archive's reading.
  Label them that way if you reuse them.
- **Check the date:** forecasts and budgets change (the Governor's budget is due Dec. 1, 2026). Each page shows when
  it was last updated.
- **Ask in plain language:** paste this into a new Claude chat, and Claude will set itself up to answer your
  questions from the archive, with sources:

    > Please set me up using https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pietvanzoen/fund-oregon-schools/main/SETUP.md

- **Have a question the site doesn't answer?** Email it to
  [hello@fundoregonschools.org](mailto:hello@fundoregonschools.org). Open questions are listed on
  [What's being decided](decisions.md); we describe who asked, never name them.
- **Found a gap or a mistake?** [Tell us](#spot-a-mistake-or-have-a-source-to-add).

## Who runs this

A volunteer group of Oregon public school parents maintains this archive. Our children are in Oregon public schools,
including Portland Public Schools, so we have a stake in these decisions. That's why every claim links to its source:
check it rather than take our word for it, and the site records options and positions on all sides of each decision.

Contact: [hello@fundoregonschools.org](mailto:hello@fundoregonschools.org)

## How it works

- **Primary documents** (budgets, reports, bills, meeting materials) are saved in the archive. See [Sources](sources.md).
- **The analysis** (decisions, topics, quotes, promises, figures, timeline) cites those documents claim by claim. Every page on
  this site is built automatically from those files, so it always matches the archive.
- **Citation links:** <span class="cite cite-src">📄 Title, p.N</span> opens a state or legislative document at the cited page.
  <span class="cite cite-news">📰 Outlet</span> opens a news story. <span class="cite cite-pps">🏫 PPS archive</span>
  opens the Portland Public Schools research archive.
- <span class="cite cite-der">🧮 our sum</span> and <span class="cite cite-der">🧮 our estimate</span> mark figures
  this archive calculated itself; the link shows the method and inputs. They are not official numbers.
- **Analysis** boxes, tags and sections mark interpretation, kept apart from what the documents say.
- Quotes are verbatim. Members of the public are described, not named.

## How research is added and checked

This process is still changing. This is an outline of how it works today.

- **Adding documents.** A document is added by recording its web address. An automated job downloads it and logs
  where it came from, when, and a fingerprint of the file. Original documents are never edited. If an agency later
  revises a document, the earlier version is kept next to the new one.
- **Writing the analysis.** The analysis is written with the help of AI tools, working from the archived documents.
  Every factual claim has to cite the document and page it comes from. Our own calculations are kept separate and
  show their method and inputs.
- **Checking it.** Before any change is published:
    - Automated checks confirm that every citation points to an archived document and that every figure we
      calculated still matches its inputs.
    - Independent reviews check that quotes are word for word and on the cited page, that no claim says more than
      its source, that the framing is fair to everyone named, and that the text is clear.
    - A maintainer reads and approves every change. The full history of changes is public on
      [GitHub](https://github.com/pietvanzoen/fund-oregon-schools).

Even with these checks, mistakes can get through. That's why every claim links to its source. If you find one,
[tell us](#spot-a-mistake-or-have-a-source-to-add).
