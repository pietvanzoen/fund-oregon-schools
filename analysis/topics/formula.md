# The funding formula: how the State School Fund is shared

Decisions this informs: `decisions.md#formula-2027`. Open research: #17, #18, #19, #25.

## What the sources say

- The formula in ORS chapter 327 divides the State School Fund among districts [SRC:legislature/ors/ors327.html].
- **Special education cap.** The formula adds weight for students eligible for special education only up to 11% of
  a district's enrollment without review and approval by the Department of Education [SRC:legislature/ors/ors327.html].
- HB 2953 (2025) as introduced would remove the cap [SRC:legislature/bills/2025R1_HB2953_introduced.pdf p.1].
- **Transportation.** The state reimburses an approved share of district transportation costs
  [SRC:legislature/ors/ors327.html].
- **Charters.** A sponsoring district must pass through at least 80% (K-8) or 95% (9-12) of its General Purpose
  Grant per ADMw to a public charter school it sponsors, virtual ones included [SRC:legislature/ors/ors338.html]; see
  `topics/virtual-charters.md`.
- Gov. Kotek's second-term platform includes revisiting enrollment-based funding [NEWS:NEWS-20260917-opb-1].
- At a Nov 6, 2026 press conference she said, as quoted by KATU: "Maybe enrollment is the wrong
  marker. That was established almost 30 years ago. Maybe it has to be student need." She called it "a longer
  conversation than what is happening in districts right now" [NEWS:NEWS-2026-katu-kotek-pps-1].

## Analysis

Still to research: HB 2953's final status (#17), how closures change transportation costs (#18), the English learner
and poverty weights (#19), and the 2027 reform proposals (#25).

Kotek has twice raised enrollment-based funding (her platform and the Nov 6 press conference). The Nov 6 remarks name
a possible direction ("student need") but no design, cost or timeline, and she framed it as a longer conversation. If a formula change is
proposed in 2027, who chairs the Education committees and the Ways and Means Education Subcommittee will shape whether
it gets a hearing. Those 2027 assignments were not yet public: on Nov 22, 2026 a web search of oregonlegislature.gov
found no 2027 committee assignment memos, only 2026 ones. Of the 2026 Ways and Means Education
Subcommittee co-chairs, Sen. Sollman will not return (she lost her May 2026 primary [NEWS:NEWS-20260521-wweek-1]), so
at least the Senate co-chair seat will change. The House's 2027 Ways and Means co-chair had not been named in the
archive's sources as of Nov 22, 2026. #107 picks up the 2027 assignment memos once they are posted.
