# Oregon K-12 funding: the research archive

*Following the money behind Oregon's schools.*

A research archive on Oregon K-12 funding, focused on the 2027-29 budget. It collects the primary documents and
cites every claim, so parents, reporters and anyone else can check facts and build their own materials.

Run by Oregon public school parents. [About this site](about.md)

- 🗳️ **What's being decided:** the Governor's budget, the State School Fund amount, reserves and the cost model, with the options on the table → `decisions.md`
- 💰 **How much:** the State School Fund against the state's own estimates of need → `topics/quality-education-model.md`
- ⚖️ **How it's shared:** the funding formula, its weights and caps → `topics/formula.md`
- 🎯 **What it buys:** results, outcome targets and accountability → `topics/results.md`

## Key numbers for 2027-29

- The Governor's recommended budget is due Dec. 1, 2026 [SRC:legislature/ors/ors291.html].
- **State School Fund, adopted by the Legislature for 2025-27:** $11.36 billion [SRC:fiscal/lfo/2025-07_lab-summary-2025-27.pdf p.1].
  The State School Fund is the primary source of state funding for K-12 public education
  [SRC:gov/press/2024_ssf-csl-proposal.html].
- **State School Fund current service level, 2027-29:** $12.56 billion, the amount needed in the next two-year budget
  to continue existing authorized programs [SRC:gov/press/2024_ssf-csl-proposal.html], as estimated by the Department of
  Administrative Services when the Quality Education Commission's 2026 report was published
  [SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.103] [SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.117].
- **Quality Education Commission estimate for 2027-29, to keep 2025-27 service levels:** $13.78 billion in core
  state funding, which counts the State School Fund and other state money, in its 2026 report [SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.103]
  [SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.117].
- **Quality Education Commission estimate for 2027-29, to meet the state's education goals:** $16.21 billion in
  core state funding, in its 2026 report [SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.117].
- The commission leaves what counts as adequate, and which funding counts toward it, to the Legislature
  [SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.117].
- Analysis: the two State School Fund figures cover the fund alone, and the commission's two estimates include other
  state money, so compare like with like: `decisions.md#ssf-2027-29` explains.
- The Education Stability Fund, one of the state's two general reserve accounts, is projected at $1.271 billion at
  the end of 2025-27 [SRC:fiscal/oea/2026-12_forecast.pdf p.41].
