# Gov. Tina Kotek

Governor of Oregon; reelected November 2026 [NEWS:NEWS-20261106-occ-1]. Submits the Governor's Recommended
Budget for 2027-29 in December [NEWS:NEWS-2026-salemreporter-1].

## On school funding
- Proposed an $11.36B State School Fund for 2025-27, an 11% increase [NEWS:NEWS-20241203-osba-1].
- Proposed raising the 2025-27 SSF current service level by about $515M over the original calculation
  [SRC:gov/press/2024_ssf-csl-proposal.html].
- Said in April 2026 she didn't expect extra school money beyond the current budget [NEWS:NEWS-20260430-wweek-1].
- After reelection, declined to commit to how she'd pay for her school goals and repeated her opposition to using
  one-time reserves for ongoing costs [NEWS:NEWS-20261106-occ-1].
- On her 2027-29 budget: "my budget will be prioritizing education and particularly summer investments again"
  [NEWS:NEWS-2026-salemreporter-1].

## On school outcomes (2026 platform and actions)
- Instructional time at the national average by the end of a second term; cut chronic absenteeism; tougher
  diploma requirements; literacy; universal preschool; revisit enrollment-based funding [NEWS:NEWS-20260917-opb-1].
- Executive Order 26-06 (Apr 16, 2026) barring cuts to instructional time and requiring 2024-25 levels to be
  restored by 2027-28 [NEWS:NEWS-20260416-wweek-1] [SRC:gov/press/2026-04-16_eo-26-06-instructional-time.html].
- Introduced SB 141, the 2025 Education Accountability Act [SRC:ode/accountability/2025-12_sb141-progress-report.pdf].

## On the Education Stability Fund
- Using it requires a governor's emergency declaration and a three-fifths legislative vote
  [NEWS:NEWS-20261102-opb-1]. She has resisted one-time money for ongoing costs [NEWS:NEWS-20261106-occ-1].
- April 2026: an FAQ released with EO 26-06 suggested she was open to using the fund to help districts with
  one-time costs of complying with the order [NEWS:NEWS-20260416-ktvz-1].
- October 2026: KATU reports she rejected tapping one-time state reserves for PPS [NEWS:NEWS-2026-katu-kotek-pps-1].
- Analysis: her stated openness in April (for her own mandate) versus refusal in October is worth confirming
  from the FAQ itself before using it publicly.

## On PPS closures
- Oct 13, 2026 letter to Superintendent Armstrong and Board Chair Wang: urged PPS to take its time but did not ask
  it to withdraw its plans [NEWS:NEWS-20261014-opb-1].
- "Portland needs to do everything it possibly can to cut administrative costs and other things before it talks
  about closing schools." [NEWS:NEWS-2026-katu-kotek-pps-1]
