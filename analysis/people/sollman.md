# Sen. Janeen Sollman

Official page: <https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/sollman>

Democrat, Senate District 15 (Forest Grove, Cornelius, Hillsboro, Rock Creek)
[SRC:legislature/members/sollman.html]. Senate co-chair of the Ways and Means Subcommittee on Education
[SRC:legislature/appointments/2026_senate-committee-assignments.pdf p.7] and co-chair of the Joint Committee on Public
Education Appropriation [SRC:legislature/olis/2025I1/310695_jpea.pdf p.3].
**Not in the 2027 Legislature:** she lost the May 19, 2026 Democratic primary to Myrna Muñoz
[NEWS:NEWS-20260521-wweek-1] [NEWS:NEWS-20260529-opb-1]. Her seat on
the subcommittee will go to someone else; she's profiled here because her positions shaped the 2025-27 budget and the
current debate over the QEM.

## Record
- Senate carrier of SB 141 B (2025 accountability act); voted yes in Ways and Means
  [SRC:legislature/bills/2025R1_SB141_budget-report.pdf p.1].
- On the 2025-27 SSF: "Increasing funding while providing clear expectations and ongoing oversight will help ensure
  our students and educators are getting the support they need." [SRC:legislature/press/2025-05-28_record-k12-funding.pdf p.1]
- Same release: "Over the years, Oregon has made significant investments into our K-12 schools, but success stories
  remain too few and far between" [SRC:legislature/press/2025-05-28_record-k12-funding.pdf p.2].
- Co-chaired the JPEA when it reported that 2025-27 funding "effectively closes the funding gap"
  [SRC:legislature/olis/2025I1/310695_jpea.pdf p.4]. Led SB 1555 with Rep. Ruiz (see `ruiz.md`).
- June 2026: joined Republicans in voting against three of Kotek's Quality Education Commission nominees:
  "The question before us isn't whether these are good people, it's about process." [NEWS:NEWS-20260617-occ-1]
