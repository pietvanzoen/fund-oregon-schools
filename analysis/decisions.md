# What's being decided

The choices on Oregon K-12 funding that someone will make by a date, with the baseline and options on the table, who
stands where and when, and what this archive can answer so far. The tables are built from the archive's decision
files; the text is cited like any analysis. A due date for a decision by the Legislature is the latest the 2027
session can run. Background: `topics/measures-5-and-50.md` explains why these are state decisions.

## The Governor's recommended budget for 2027-29 {#grb-2027-29}

- State law sets Dec 1 of even-numbered years for a governor continuing in office to submit a budget
  [SRC:legislature/ors/ors291.html]; Kotek was reelected in November [NEWS:NEWS-20261106-occ-1].
- It's built on the Nov 18 revenue forecast [SRC:fiscal/oea/2026-12_forecast.pdf p.33]. News reported the forecast
  added about $245M for 2027-29 [NEWS:NEWS-20261118-klcc-1]; the exact figure is still to confirm (#5).
- Kotek has said her budget will prioritize education and summer learning [NEWS:NEWS-2026-salemreporter-1], and
  after the election declined to commit to how she would pay for her school goals [NEWS:NEWS-20261106-occ-1].
- Analysis: when the budget is released, compare its State School Fund amount with each figure in the comparison set
  for the next decision, not with one benchmark alone.

## The State School Fund amount for 2027-29 {#ssf-2027-29}

- The Joint Committee on Ways and Means writes the budget; Sen. Kate Lieber returns as Senate co-chair
  [SRC:legislature/press/2026-11-13_senate-dems-2027-leadership.pdf p.1]. Other 2027 roles aren't announced yet.
- The session begins Jan 19, 2027 [SRC:legislature/ors/ors171.html] and can run to June 27
  [SRC:legislature/constitution/oregon-constitution.html] [SRC:reference/2026-09-30_opdc-navigating-2027-session.pdf].
  Revenue forecasts are tentatively set for Feb 24 and May 12 [SRC:fiscal/oea/2027_forecast-calendar.pdf].
- The current service level is $12.56B, the full Quality Education Model $16.21B, and the adopted 2025-27 amount
  $11.36B [SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.103] [SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.117]
  [SRC:fiscal/lfo/2025-07_lab-summary-2025-27.pdf p.1].
- The Quality Education Commission leaves the question of what is adequate, and which funding counts, to the
  Legislature [SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.117].
- The positions of the Oregon Education Association, OSBA and COSA on the 2027-29 amount aren't archived yet (#26).
- Analysis: the current service level is the State School Fund alone, while both Quality Education Model figures count
  other state money too, so the like-for-like gap between the two model scenarios is the status quo to the full
  model. See `topics/quality-education-model.md`.

## Drawing on the Education Stability Fund in 2027-29 {#esf-draw-2027-29}

- The fund is projected at $1,271M at the end of 2025-27 [SRC:fiscal/oea/2026-12_forecast.pdf p.41].
- Withdrawal takes a three-fifths vote in each chamber plus either an economic trigger or a governor's emergency
  declaration [SRC:fiscal/lro/2025-11_reserve-funds.pdf p.1].
- State reserves are currently 9.7% [SRC:fiscal/oea/2026-12_forecast_slides.pdf p.37]; the projected 2027-29 balances
  ($2.9B Rainy Day Fund, $1.6B Education Stability Fund) would be 10.6% of the General Fund projection
  [SRC:fiscal/oea/2026-12_forecast.pdf p.41].
- In 2021, a school funding plan from House and Senate Democrats relied on $200M from the fund
  [SRC:legislature/press/2021-05-11_democrats-9.3b-school-funding-plan.pdf p.1]; whether it passed as proposed is still
  to confirm.
- Rep. Ruiz frames his proposal as immediate relief while longer-term reform is developed
  [SRC:legislature/social/2026-11-19_ruiz-instagram-osba-panel.txt].
- Analysis: every option here needs the same vote and trigger. Kotek and Lieber object on the record to one-time money
  for ongoing costs; PERS contributions are an ongoing cost, so the objection may apply to both PERS proposals, while
  supporters describe a one-time draw as a bridge until lasting reform. Sanchez's stated objection is mainly timing:
  saving the fund for worse years. The stances recorded so far were about a draw in 2025-27, not 2027-29. See
  `topics/reserves.md`.

## Changing or replacing the Quality Education Model {#cost-model-2027}

- A legislative concept to move the cost model to the Legislative Policy and Research Office
  [SRC:legislature/olis/2025I1/311607_jpea-legislative-concepts.pdf p.1] became SB 1555 (2026), which would also
  abolish the Quality Education Commission [SRC:legislature/bills/2026R1_SB1555_overview.html].
- A 2026 overhaul effort led by Ruiz and Sen. Sollman was unsuccessful [NEWS:NEWS-20260617-occ-1].
- Analysis: a new cost model would also change the benchmarks the other decisions are measured against. See
  `topics/quality-education-model.md`.
