# How Oregon school funding works

This guide follows the money for day-to-day school operations in six steps: where it comes from, how much the state
sets, how it's divided among districts, what districts add, what it's meant to buy, and who decides. Each step links
to the page that holds the documents behind it.

<!-- site: visual dollar-to-school -->

## 1. Where the money comes from {#where-the-money-comes-from}

State money is about two-thirds of the money the funding formula shares out, and local revenue, mostly property taxes,
is the rest, according to the Legislative Fiscal Office's 2025-27 budget review
[SRC:legislature/olis/2025R1/293759_ssf-budget-review.pdf p.1]. The state's part of the State School Fund is General
Fund money, from the state's main budget account, along with Lottery Funds, marijuana tax revenue and Corporate
Activity Tax revenue [SRC:legislature/olis/2025R1/293759_ssf-budget-review.pdf p.1].

The state's share of school operating money grew in the 1990s, as voters limited property taxes, according to the
Oregon Encyclopedia [SRC:reference/oregon-encyclopedia_measure-5.html]; step 4 covers those limits. The archive
doesn't yet have a full split of today's school money between the state, local taxes and the federal government, or a
source on how federal money outside the formula reaches districts.

That state share is the State School Fund, whose amount the Legislature sets
[SRC:legislature/olis/2025R1/293759_ssf-budget-review.pdf p.1]; step 2 covers how.

## 2. How the state decides how much {#how-the-state-decides-how-much}

The Legislature sets the State School Fund amount in each two-year budget; for 2025-27 it adopted $11.36 billion
[SRC:fiscal/lfo/2025-07_lab-summary-2025-27.pdf p.1]. The baseline the archive compares 2027-29 proposals against, on
`decisions.md`, is the State School Fund's current service level, the amount needed to continue existing programs
[SRC:gov/press/2024_ssf-csl-proposal.html]. The Department of Administrative Services works it out by adjusting the
last budget's State School Fund spending for inflation and enrollment [SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.100]. The
department's 2027-29 figure is $12.56 billion, the estimate available when the Quality Education Commission published
its 2026 report in August 2026 [SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.1] [SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.103]
[SRC:ode/qec/2026_qem_report.pdf p.117].

## 3. How it's shared among districts {#how-its-shared-among-districts}

Once the Legislature sets the amount, the formula in chapter 327 of the Oregon Revised Statutes divides the State
School Fund among districts [SRC:legislature/ors/ors327.html]. Each district's amount is its General Purpose Grant
plus transportation and high-cost disability grants, minus its local revenue, which is mostly property taxes
[SRC:legislature/ors/ors327.html] [SRC:legislature/olis/2025R1/293759_ssf-budget-review.pdf p.1]:

<!-- site: summary formula -->

Because local revenue is subtracted [SRC:legislature/ors/ors327.html], step 4 turns to what a district can raise
locally and keep.

## 4. What districts can add locally, and the limits {#what-districts-can-add-locally-and-the-limits}

A district's voters can approve a local option levy, an extra property tax for day-to-day costs
[SRC:fiscal/dor/2009-06_brief-history-property-taxation.pdf p.4]. Up to a cap, that money isn't subtracted from the
district's State School Fund grant, but its regular property taxes are [SRC:legislature/ors/ors327.html]:

Step 5 turns from how much money there is to what it's meant to buy.

## 5. What the money is supposed to buy {#what-the-money-is-supposed-to-buy}

Some state money comes as grants paid in addition to the State School Fund, with purposes set in law
[SRC:legislature/ors/ors327.html]. Of the state grants the Legislature's Joint Committee on Public Education
Appropriation counted as core K-12 funding for 2025-27, the largest is the Student Investment Account
[SRC:legislature/olis/2025I1/310959_jpea-adequacy-report.pdf p.29]:

## 6. Who decides, and when {#who-decides-and-when}

The Governor sends the Legislature a recommended budget by Dec. 1 of each even-numbered year, unless a new governor
takes office the next January [SRC:legislature/ors/ors291.html]. In 2025, the Education Subcommittee of the
Legislature's Joint Committee on Ways and Means took up the State School Fund budget bill, Senate Bill 5516
[SRC:legislature/olis/2025R1/307212_sb5516-ssf-work-session.pdf p.1]. Who holds each role:
[Who decides](people/people.csv). What's being decided now, with each option and where it stands:
[What's being decided](decisions.md).
