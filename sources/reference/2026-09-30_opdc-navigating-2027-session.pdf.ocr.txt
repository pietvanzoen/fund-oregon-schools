Version control matters

“What happens next?”

Hearings, testimony,
amendments, fiscal questions
and work-session votes can
materially change a bill.

Introduced, amended, engrossed
and enrolled versions are not
interchangeable.

Use scheduled events + measure
history + committee agenda to
identify the next procedural step.
