Oregon Senate Republican Caucus Welcomes New Senators
Following Swearing-In Ceremony
SALEM, Ore. – The Oregon Senate Republican Caucus proudly welcomes its newest
members as Bruce Starr (R-Dundee), Mike McLane (R-Powell Butte), Todd Nash (REnterprise), Diane Linthicum (R-Beatty), and Noah Robinson (R-Cave Junction)
were officially sworn into office today. These leaders bring a wealth of knowledge and a
shared commitment to serving Oregonians and ensuring the voices of their communities
are heard in Salem.
“Our new senators represent the diverse values and priorities of Oregonians,” said
Senate Republican Leader Daniel Bonham (R-The Dalles). “Bruce, Mike, Diane, Noah,
and Todd are dedicated to championing policies that address our state’s most pressing
challenges, from housing to public safety and beyond. We’re excited to have them join
our team and look forward to working together to deliver results for our constituents.”
As these new senators begin their service, the Caucus also expresses heartfelt gratitude
to the outgoing senators whose terms ended today: Tim Knopp (R-Bend), Bill Hansell
(R-Athena), Lynn Findley (R-Vale), Art Robinson (R-Cave Junction), Brian Boquist
(R-Dallas), and Dennis Linthicum (R-Beatty).
“Each of these senators has left a lasting legacy in the Oregon Senate, not only for the
communities they served but also for me personally,” said Bonham. “As colleagues and
mentors, they set an example of integrity, dedication, and leadership that I will always
carry with me. Their contributions have shaped our state for the better, and we wish them
all the best in their future endeavors.”
With today’s swearing-in, the Caucus is eager to begin the new legislative session united
and ready to serve.
