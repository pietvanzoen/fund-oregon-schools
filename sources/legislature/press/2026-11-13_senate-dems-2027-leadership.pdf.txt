Oregon Senate Democrats Unite Around Leadership Team for
2027 Legislative Session
Gleneden Beach, Ore. – Oregon Senate Democrats today chose eight of their peers to serve
as caucus leaders for the 2027 session, roles that shepherd legislative priorities on behalf of
Oregonians.
Democrats selected presiding officers to run for re-election by a majority vote of the full,
bipartisan Oregon Senate when the legislature convenes on Monday, January 11 to organize
ahead of the start of the 2027 legislative session:
• Senate President-designate Rob Wagner (D - Lake Oswego)
• Senate President Pro Tempore-designate: James Manning (D - Eugene)
“I’m privileged to again represent the Senate Democrats as their choice for Oregon’s next
Senate President. The 2027-2029 biennium will bring a range of challenges, and I’m grateful for
the caucus’s confidence,” said Senate President Rob Wagner. “I look forward to the upcoming
session and working with all members of the chamber to successfully address the priorities that
mean the most to those who call Oregon home.” Senator Wagner has served in the Oregon
Senate since 2018 and has been presiding officer since 2023.
In a caucus vote that becomes effective immediately, Senate Democrats assembled the
leadership team for the caucus:
• Senate Majority Leader: Anthony Broadman (Bend)
• Senate Deputy Majority Leader: Wlnsvey Campos (Aloha)
• Senate Floor Manager: Sara Gelser Blouin (Corvallis, Albany & S. Salem)
• Senate Floor Manager: Lisa Reynolds (Portland)
• Senate Assistant Majority Leader: Khanh Pham (Outer SE & NE Portland)
• Senate Assistant Majority Leader: Courtney Neron Misslin (Tigard, King City &
Wilsonville)
• Joint Ways and Means Committee Co-Chair: Kate Lieber (Beaverton & SW Portland)

“Oregon is facing serious challenges, and working families are struggling to keep up with the
rising cost of living,” said Senate Majority Leader Anthony Broadman, who was first elected
to the Oregon Senate in 2024. “Voters entrusted us with the largest Democratic majority in
decades, and our first priority must be making life more affordable for Oregon families. We will
meet this moment with urgency to deliver real results that make a difference in the lives of
Oregonians.”
Senator Broadman, who represents Bend, Redmond, Sisters and much of Deschutes County
in the Oregon Senate, is the first Oregon Senate Democratic Leader from east of the Cascade
Range since 1980. He has been an attorney for nearly two decades, representing Tribal
governments and small businesses. Senator Broadman also has served as Chief Judge of the
Warm Springs Court of Appeals and chaired the Oregon State Bar’s Indian Law Section. Before
his election to the Senate in 2024, Sen. Broadman served four years on the Bend City Council,
including as mayor pro tem. He and his wife, Kate, are raising three daughters in Bend.
In the 2026 legislative session, Sen. Broadman helped champion expansion of Oregon’s Earned
Income Tax Credit to cut taxes for more than 200,000 working households. His priorities are to
lower the cost of living, to build an economy where local businesses can grow and workers can
thrive, and to make Oregon’s schools and communities safer.
