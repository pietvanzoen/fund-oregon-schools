Chair Frederick, Vice-Chair Weber, and Members of the Senate Education Committee:
My name is Bruce Kevan. I serve as a member of the LaGrande School Board, but I write to
you today in my individual capacity as a retired educator and school principal. As you
debate SB 1555 and consider changing how schools should be judged and funded, please
consider the following:
•

The best decisions are made closest to the action. Who knows best what the
successes and challenges a school district has? The staff, families and students do.
Please don’t move the accountability and funding discussions another level away.

•

The best decisions are made when based on quality data. The metrics currently
used are not a valid sample. For instance, if students can opt out of the
assessments, the resulting data never reﬂects truth. As you consider the standards
for measuring student success, ask the school districts what data they have and
can provide and how they measure success locally.

•

Funding of schools has struggled to keep up with the cost of a quality education.
But, much has been given and rightly much can be expected. At this point if funding
does not keep pace and expectations remain or increase, how can the Legislature
be considering cuts to education? As you consider funding in conjunction with
quality education, please hold the schools harmless and pay for the results you
want.

I’ve seen many new ideas come and go in my time in Oregon’s school system. It may be
time to revise our statewide educational goals and our current process for determining how
much money is needed in the system, but we shouldn’t rush that conversation. We should
include the perspective of those with experience in the ﬁeld.
Thank you for your consideration.
