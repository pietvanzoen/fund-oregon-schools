Good afternoon members of the Joint Committee on Public Education Appropriations,
I am attaching a revised version of this committee’s draft report. This revision contains the
following changes:

1. Page 9 on the document (page 15 of the PDF): the three funding alternatives have
been modified to better reflect the full scope of the QEC’s recommendations. The
original list contained three parts of their first alternative rather than all three
alternatives.

2. Page 33 on the document (page 39 of the PDF): “between” has been changed to
“among” in paragraph seven.

3. Page 34 on the document (page 40 of the PDF): a sentence regarding school district
uptake was removed from paragraph nine. The committee did not actually hear a
presentation on that topic due to challenges with applying the QEM’s recommended
staffing levels to Oregon schools, since most Oregon schools are significantly smaller
than the QEM’s model schools.
This revised draft will be posted on OLIS, along with this email message.
