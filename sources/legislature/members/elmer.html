Greeting
Edit

​Welcome to House District 24

Thank you for checking out my Legislative Website!

It is an honor to serve House District 24 in the House of Representatives. I have lived in this district my whole life, and will work hard to be a voice for our community in Salem.​

My office will communicate with you about the work being done in Salem, and we will ask for your input. We care about what you think, and appreciate your ideas in solving the problems we face together.​ Please use to the link to the right to sign up for my newsletter to get weekly updates while in session or monthly updates throughout the year.

I encourage you to explore the Legislative website for more information on the concerns and issues that impact our lives. It is important that you and I work as a team to have the greatest impact on the outcomes of this year's legislative session.

These issues matter now, and for the next generation. I welcome your ideas and solutions, and work with you as we walk through this process. Please feel free to reach out to me or my staff directly at any point. We will be happy to hear from you!

I hope you find the information found here useful and I encourage you to take an active role in the legislative process. You can learn more by reading about the legislative process and how an idea becomes law.

Thank you!​
