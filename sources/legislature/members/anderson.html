Greeting
Edit

​Welcome to my Legislative Website.

Thank you for visiting my legislative website. I am proud to be your Senator in District 5 which serves all of Lincoln County and parts of ​​Benton, Lane, Douglas and Coos counties.

I am honored to represent my constituents and look forward to bringing our issues to the Senate while bridging all parts of the legislature so our voice is heard.  It is in our vested interest to remind others that one policy does not fit all corners of Oregon.

I am passionate about improving our education system, housing, infrastructure, the economy for our workforce and improving our healthcare system, including mental health.

I hope to hear from you through the office email and phone. Feel free to contact my office to share your thoughts. Sign up on this page for our newsletter in the "e-subscribe" box to the right. Please include your email address and phone number when contacting me.

Thank you.

​Dick Anderson
