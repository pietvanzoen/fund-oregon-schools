Greeting
Edit
Thank you for visiting. I am proud to have the honor of serving as Senate President and to serve as the Senator for District 19, which includes Lake Oswego, West Linn, Tualatin, Southwest Portland, Rivergrove, and Durham.

I am passionate about investing in the future of Oregon — from funding for schools and workforce training, to protecting our seniors and most vulnerable community members.
 
I want to hear from you! Please reach out and share your thoughts. You can contact my office at 503-986-1600. To receive the latest information, follow me on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and subscribe to my electronic newsletter by entering your email in the "e-subscribe" box on the right of your screen.
 
  Thank you,
​ 
  Robert A. Wagner​
