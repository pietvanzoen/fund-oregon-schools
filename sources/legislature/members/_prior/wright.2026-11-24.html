​Welcome to my Legislative Website.


Friends and Neighbors, 

Welcome! It is an honor to represent the people of House District 9, spanning from the north end of Yachats to the south end of Coos Bay along the beautiful Oregon Coast. It also encompasses the communities of Cheshire, Florence, Lakeside, Mapleton, North Bend, Reedsport, Scottsburg, and Triangle Lake.

Here, I encourage you to share your thoughts on important issues that come before the Oregon Legislature. It is of utmost importance that your voices be heard. 

​Resources

Please do not hesistate to contact my legislative office​ at (503) 986-1409 or Rep.BoomerWright@oregonlegislature.gov.

To learn about agendas, bills, committees, and measure history, explore the Oregon Legislative Information System (OLIS) here​. 

To learn how to submit both oral and written testimony, click here.​

Thank you for visiting my legislative website. I hope you find the information provided here useful as I encourage you to take an active role in the legislative process. My hope is that, together, we can make improvements for the Coast and the State of Oregon. 

Blessings,​

​

Boomer Wright
