Greeting

Edit

​Friends, neighbors, and fellow Oregonians,

I am proud to represent Tigard, Metzger, and South Beaverton as a State Representative and am honored to have been elected by my colleagues who represent other cities and towns across our state to be House Majority Leader.

I was born and raised in Oregon and love this state deeply. I believe Oregon should be the best place in America to live and raise a family, with world-class schools, a clean environment, affordable housing and healthcare, sustainable infrastructure, and abundant economic opportunity. I am committed to advancing this vision.

As a State Representative and House Majority Leader, I work for you. We may not always agree, but I’m guided by the voices and experiences of our community in all the work I do. I grew up attending Tigard-Tualatin schools, playing soccer at Cook Park, and hanging out with friends at Washington Square. So whether you have concerns, comments, ideas, or complaints, I want to hear from you. Oregonians deserve to have their voice heard in the legislature.

My legislative website is a resource for you to stay updated on what is happening in Salem. I hope you find the information here useful and I encourage you to take an active role in your state legislature. Learn more by reading about the

legislative process and

how an idea becomes law. Engage with my office by sending me an email at

rep.benbowman@oregonlegislature.gov or calling my office at 503-986-1425. To stay up to date with what our office is working on, you can subscribe to my newsletter​, or follow me on

Facebook​,

Instagram, or

Twitter.

Best,

​Representative Ben Bowman
