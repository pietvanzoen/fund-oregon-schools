Rural Northwest Oregon Updates Volume 64

Oregon State Legislature sent this bulletin at 03/11/2026 11:34 AM PDT

Replies to this message are sent to an unmonitored mailbox.
To contact me, please click here: Sen​.SuzanneWeber@oregonlegislature.gov

From the District

The 2026 short legislative session wrapped up with some important wins for our coastal communities.

In this From the District session ending update, I share progress on key priorities, including time change legislation and the passage of HB 4148, which helps ensure transient lodging tax dollars are fairly shared with the communities hosting Oregon’s visitors.

I also talk about SB 1561, which streamlines the rebuilding process for families whose homes are destroyed by natural disasters, and funding secured for projects in Warrenton and Astoria.

Take a moment to watch for a quick update on what these efforts mean for District 16.

While the 2026 short legislative session has come to a close, the work of serving our communities certainly does not stop. The best part of this job is hearing directly from the people of District 16 and learning about the issues that matter most to you.

If there is something happening in your community that you think our office should know about, please do not hesitate to reach out. You can always email me directly at Sen.SuzanneWeber@oregonlegislature.gov.

It is truly an honor to serve the people of our coastal communities. Thank you for staying engaged and for allowing me to be your voice in Salem.
