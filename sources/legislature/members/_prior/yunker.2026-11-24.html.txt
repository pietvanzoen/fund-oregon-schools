Greeting

Edit

​​​​​​​​​​​Welcome, friends! It is a privilege and an honor to represent the citizens of Josephine County House District 3 in the Oregon State Legislature.

Located in southwest Oregon, Josephine County is known for its pioneer tradition, beautiful scenery and year-round great weather, and abundant outdoor recreation.

As your State Representative, I am dedicated to promoting common-sense, conservative policies grounded in limited government, fiscal responsibility, and free-market principles. These are the proven pillars that allow our families and local businesses to thrive.

A lifetime of service

Before serving in Salem, I spent over two decades in uniform across the U.S. Navy, the U.S. Marine Corps, and the Army National Guard. I bring that same unshakable commitment to duty, discipline, and defense of our constitutional rights to the Capitol every single day.

My priorities

My priorities are fourfold.

Fiscal responsibility & economic ppportunity​

Public safety & supporting law enforcement

Defending constitutional freedoms & family values

Serve the needs of my constituents in Grants Pass & Josephine County

Fiscal responsibility & economic opportunity​

Government must live within its means so local families and businesses can thrive. I am committed to fighting wasteful spending, lowering taxes, and demanding strict budget accountability. By pairing fiscal discipline with regulatory relief, we will unleash opportunity and build a more prosperous Southern Oregon.​​

Public safety & supporting law enforcement​

Safe communities are the bedrock of a prosperous society. I stand firmly with our local law enforcement officers and advocate for a justice system that prioritizes accountability, rejects "catch-and-release" policies, and keeps our neighborhoods secure.​

Defending constitutional freedoms & family values

Our constitutional rights must be protected without apology. I will always stand firm in defense of the Second Amendment and advocate for traditional family values, parental rights, and local control over our education systems.
​

Serve my constituents in Grants Pass & Josephine County

State government works best when citizens stay informed and engaged. You can find out who represents you here. You can track and stay involved in the legislative process by using the Oregon Legislative Information System (OLIS) here.

Please subscribe to my electronic newsletter by entering your email in the "e-Subscribe" box on your screen.

I am here to serve you and look forward to hearing your concerns and suggestions!

Please be in touch with me via email at Rep.DwayneYunker@oregonlegislature.gov, over the phone at 503.986.1403, or by stopping by my office in the Oregon Capitol Building, 900 Court St. NE, H-377, Salem, OR 97301.

Sincerely,

Dwayne Yunker
