Greeting
Edit

​Welcome to my Legislative Website


Thank you for visiting my legislative web page. I am honored to represent Senate District 14, which includes Beaverton and Southwest Portland. I am a mother of two, a breast cancer survivor, a lawyer and a former Portland Community College instructor. I understand that those with power must use it justly and wisely - and this sentiment has always grounded my work.


As a State Senator, I am committed to serving you and creating an Oregon that works for everyone.  Our communities are facing unprecedented challenges – from the homelessness crisis and community safety to wildfires and rising prices. As your State Senator, I will work to ensure the state government is solving the most pressing problems facing oregonians.


We all deserve to live with dignity in safe, sustainable communities free from racism and discrimination with equal access to quality health care, world-class schools, and secure, good-paying jobs. 


I strive to represent the interests of the entire district and I value your input, questions, and concerns. You can contact my office by phone at (503) 986-1714, or by email at sen.katelieber@oregonlegislature.gov. To stay updated, please subscribe to my newsletter​.


Best,​

Senator Kate Lieber​
