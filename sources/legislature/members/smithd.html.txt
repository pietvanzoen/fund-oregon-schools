​Welcome to my Legislative Website.

Dear Friends,

Thank you for visiting my legislative website. I am honored and humbled to represent the citizens of Senate District 1 which includes Curry and parts of Coos and Douglas Counties, Southwest Oregon's Fish, Farm and Forestry District.

For the 2026 Legislative Year, I have been assigned the following committees:

Vice Chair - Senate Committee On Energy and Environment

Senate Committee On Veterans, Emergency Management, Federal and World Affairs

​Legislative Policy and Research

Joint Ways and Means - Public Safety Subcommittee & Natural Resources Subcommittee

Full Ways and Means Committee

​Over the various sessions here at the Oregon Legislature, I have also served as Vice Chair on the House Committee for Energy and Environment, Co Vice-Chair of the Joint Committee on Carbon Reduction, Vice-Chair of the House Economic Development Committee, and as a member on the House Agriculture, Land Use, and Natural Resources,​ Joint Ways & Means Sub Committee on Natural Resources. as well as the House Committee on Early Childhood and FamiIy Supports. I also served as the Co-Convener of the Sudden Oak Task Force, Co-Convener of the Maritime Workforce Development Task Force, Co-Convener of the Oregon Coast Trail, Co-Chair of the Oregon Legislative Sportsman's Caucus, Co-Chair of the Oregon Legislative Wine Caucus, Governor's Opioid Epidemic Task Force, appointed Senate Republican-Oregon's Climate Action Commission, appointed Senator-Oregon Environmental Restoration Council,​ Oregon Senate Delegate to PNWER (Pacific North West Economic Region), I'm also a member on the Western Legislative Forestry Task Force, Council of State Governments West (CSG West) Co-Chair Public Safety Committee, currently the CSG West Co-Chair Wildfire & Disaster Preparedness Committee ​and the Senate Board Member to the Oregon Infrastructure Finance Authority. I serve on other workgroups as needed.

My legislative office works year-round and is available to assist you with any questions or concerns that you might have about legislation, laws, disputes with state agencies, or other state related issues. Please feel free to visit my legislative office at the Capitol or contact me using the information at the top of this page.​

I encourage you to participate in the legislative process. Answering your questions and responding to your concerns is my top priority and I look forward to hearing from you. If you want to follow the progress of a bill or watch a committee hearing, you can do so by utilizing the Oregon Legislative Information System (OLIS). Click HERE to go to OLIS.

My office sends out an e-newsletter on a regular basis containing legislative and community updates. If you are interested in receiving my e-newsletter, please type your e-mail address into the "E-Subscribe" box on the right hand side of the screen.

Thank you for the honor and privilege to work with and for you as your State Senator.

Blessings,

David Brock Smith
