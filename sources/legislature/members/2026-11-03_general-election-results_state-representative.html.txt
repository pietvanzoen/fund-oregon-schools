Election Terminology

Write-in results are not reported on this site. Unofficial write-in results for statewide or state district offices may be obtained from Elections.SOS@sos.oregon.gov after election day as counties finalize write-in totals. Unofficial write-in results for county and precinct offices may be obtained from the county election office.

Election Night Results Reporting is a free service provided by the Office of Oregon Secretary of State, in cooperation with the Oregon Association of County Clerks.
