Greeting
Edit

Greetings, 

I am honored to serve you in the Oregon Legislature, where I proudly represent North and Northeast Portland. I currently serve as Co-Chair of the Joint Committee on Ways and Means and Co-Chair of the Joint Committee on Audits. I also serve on the Joint Committee on Addiction and Community Safety Response.​

I have been advocating for social justice my entire life and am proud to bring that experience and dedication to the Oregon Legislature. From serving as a foster parent to co-founding the Native American Youth and Family Center, I have seen firsthand the numerous obstacles faced by children and families in our community. I am committed to removing these obstacles by improving our behavioral health system, investing in affordable housing, and strengthening early education. Fighting for the underserved in our district and our state remains my top priority.

Please sign up for updates on our progress by entering your email address in the upper right-hand corner of this page. 

You are welcome and encouraged to reach out to my office at any time. My staff and I greatly value your participation in the legislative process and are always happy to assist you.

You can call me at (503) 986-1443 or reach me by email at Rep.TawnaSanchez@oregonlegislature.gov. 

Aishen (Thank You), ​​

Representative Tawna Sanchez
State Representative
North and Northeast Portland ​
