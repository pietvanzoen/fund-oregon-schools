Meeting Scribe: Mariah Robillard
Introductions
Chair Mooney facilitated introductions with Commissioners and ODE staff.
ODE Updates
Dr. Candice Castillo shared ODE updates including SB 141 work, noting accountability structures
to streamline how ODE supports school districts. She also shared that vendor demonstrations
are happening this week so that districts and public charters can hear from vendors and ask
questions. It was also noted that there have been federal funding decreases and most districts
will see a reduction in funds. ODE is working with districts to support with planning and
technical assistance.
Framework for 2026 QEM Report
Andrea Lockard introduced a conversation about the framework for the 2026 QEM Report. Evan
Fuller reviewed the proposed outline for the report and the Commission reviewed and
discussed their thoughts on the outline.
Best Practices Survey
Evan Fuller reviewed the Best Practices Survey results and answered questions from
Commissioners.
Leah Thorp gave an update that they had received all of the Best Practices White Papers and
would send out a synthesis as soon as possible.
QEM Writing Subcommittee
Chair Mooney explained that there was a formal vote needed to confirm a writing
subcommittee for the QEM Report. The purpose of this subcommittee would be acting as a
resource support to ODE staff as well as getting clarification and feedback and bringing it back
to the Commission to discuss.

Dana Hepper motioned to approve the subcommittee. Reed Scott-Schwalbach seconded the
motion. There was unanimous approval from Commissioners to approve the subcommittee.
Chair Mooney requested that Commissioners let her know if they are interested in joining the
subcommittee.
Wrap Up
Chair Mooney concluded and adjourned the meeting.
