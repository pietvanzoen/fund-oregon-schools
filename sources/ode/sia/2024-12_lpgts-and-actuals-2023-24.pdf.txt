2023-24 LPGTs and Actuals: Overview of Target
Achievement for 3rd Grade ELA Proficiency, 9th Grade
On-Track, Regular Attendance, Four-Year Graduation,
and Five-Year Completer Rates
In the spring and summer of 2023, Student Investment Account (SIA) grantees with more than 80 ADMr
(students) met with the ODE’s Office of Education Innovation and Improvement staff to co-develop
Longitudinal Performance Growth Targets (LPGTs) for the five common metrics. 1 The targets set covered
the 2023-24 through 2027-28 school years. Data for all five metrics have been released for 2023-24 and
we can compare the first year of targets with the actual rates.
For an analysis of the targets without actuals, including more background information about how the
targets were set, see 2023-2028 LPGTs: An Overview of the Targets.

Overview
A majority of grantees met their first year of targets for most metrics. Exceptions include baseline
targets for the Grade 3 ELA proficiency rate and gap-closing targets for the Five-Year completer rate.
Additionally, a higher rate of change is seen for the combined focal student group than for all students
in all five metrics, suggesting that the disproportionality is decreasing. The largest increases were seen in
the Regular Attendance rate, with 54 grantees surpassing their 2027-28 baseline targets.
