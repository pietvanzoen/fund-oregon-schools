Virtual Public Charter Schools and the Student Success Act

Virtual Public Charter Schools are not eligible to apply for Student Investment Account (SIA) funding
under the Student Success Act (SSA) 1. Additionally, the Department of Education must remove the
ADMw attributable to a virtual public charter school from an eligible school district’s SIA allocation 2. The
Department has determined the following public charter schools meet the definition of Virtual Public
Charter School as defined in ORS 338.005. These determinations were made using information reported
by the charter schools directly to the Department’s charter school specialist and information from the
Department’s institution database on schools that identify as full-virtual or focus-virtual. The ADMw
attributable to these schools will be removed from the SIA calculations for their sponsoring school
districts.

Virtual Charter School Status Reconsideration Process

If a charter school or their sponsoring district on this list does not believe the charter school meets the
definition of Virtual Public Charter School as defined in ORS 338.005, they may make a formal request to
ODE’s Charter School team, at ode.charterschools@ode.oregon.gov and provide the following evidence
and documentation to the Department by 11:59 p.m. on Friday, November 13, 2026.
1. The reason(s) you believe the charter school does not match the definition of Virtual Public
Charter School as defined in ORS 338.005.
2. Charter Contract.
3. Description of the charter school’s educational program, including whether the online
instructional activities integral to the academic program of the school as described in its charter
4. Detailed description of all courses the charter school offers with the following information for
each course:
a. Are instruction and content delivered primarily on a computer using the internet, other
electronic network or other technology such as CDs or DVDs?
b. Are the student and teacher in different physical locations for a majority of the
student’s instructional period while participating in the course?
c. Is the student required to be located at the physical location of a school while
participating in the course?
5. Detailed accounting of whether more than 50 percent of the core courses offered by the school
are offered at a physical location and are not online courses.
6. Detailed accounting of whether more than 50 percent of the total number of students attending
the school are receiving instructional services at a physical location and not in an online course.
7. Detailed accounting of whether more than 50 percent of the minimum number of instructional
hours required to be provided to students by the school under OAR 581-022-2320 during a
school year are provided at a physical location and not through an online course.
