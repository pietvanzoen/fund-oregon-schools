Education Accountability Act:
State and Local Performance Growth Targets
Oregon’s Education Accountability Act (SB 141) establishes a more accountable K–12
system focused on high‑quality education for all Oregon students. It sets measurable goals
to drive student success and gives the Oregon Department of Education (ODE) clearer
authority to support improvement, reinforce success, and monitor progress over time.
Recognizing that meaningful reform takes time, SB 141 focuses on steady improvement
over the next four to 12 years. Statewide Performance Growth Targets (PGTs) outline
Oregon’s long-term direction for student success, with four-year milestones that set a
shared statewide direction, anchor accountability decisions, and increase transparency.

Accountability means setting clear goals for student success,
measuring progress over time, and using that information to guide support and improvement.

Statewide Expectations and Goals
These targets are designed to guide improvement over time, using consistent statewide measures. They are based on the
science of learning, evidence-based data about education system improvement, and what some Oregon districts are already
showing is possible.

Increase K-2 and K-12 attendance to historically strong statewide levels
Achieve higher statewide performance across all other metrics
Place at or near the top nationally in attendance, graduation, and academic outcomes based on the most recent national

Local PGTs ensure that every district contributes meaningfully, based on their

These targets set a clear expectation: Outcomes must improve in meaningful
and measurable ways over time. They help districts and charter schools focus
planning, align resources, and stay accountable to their communities.

Districts and charter schools will be accountable for meeting the targets they set
year over year, beginning with the 2026-27 school year. While local targets will
differ based on district context, they’ll be aligned to the same statewide goals.
Progress towards Oregon’s statewide PGTs is achieved through collective local
progress, with districts moving in a shared statewide direction while retaining
flexibility to reflect their local priorities.

Similar District Performance Growth Targets
Districts and charter schools will set their targets based on local context and
historical data, informed by Similar District Targets set by ODE. Similar districts
are groups with shared characteristics, such as student population and
community context, to support fair and meaningful target-setting.

Support and Accountability
Under SB 141, accountability is about supporting improvement.
A strong continuum of state supports will include:
Strengthened coaching and technical assistance for all districts and charter
schools
Increasing levels of intervention when districts aren’t meeting improvement
goals
Every district and charter school – regardless of size, type, or student population
– is supported and held accountable in ways that drive continuous, measurable
improvement in student outcomes.
