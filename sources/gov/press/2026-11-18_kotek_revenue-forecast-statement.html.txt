Governor Kotek Issues Statement in Response to December Revenue Forecast

Salem, OR – Today, Governor Tina Kotek issued a statement in response to the fourth quarter revenue forecast:

“Today’s revenue forecast shows Oregon state government has steady financial footing on which to protect essential services, even as stubborn inflation persists as a result of tepid federal action. While this economic trajectory is encouraging, we must create more momentum to help families struggling to make ends meet. That means making responsible investments to lower costs, foster job growth, and create more economic opportunity across the state.”
