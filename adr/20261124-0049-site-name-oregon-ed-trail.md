# The site is called Oregon Ed Trail, at oregonedtrail.org

Status: accepted (2026-11-23, PR #TBD)

## Context
The domain fundoregonschools.org reads as a demand ("fund Oregon schools"), which works against an archive meant to
be neutral and to include every side's evidence. The old site name, Oregon K-12 Funding Research, was accurate but
dry. The main readers are Oregon parents, many of whom grew up with the Oregon Trail.

## Decision
The site is called Oregon Ed Trail, at oregonedtrail.org, with the tagline "Following the money behind Oregon's
schools." Contact moves to hello@oregonedtrail.org. The repository keeps its name, so links and clones keep working.

## Options weighed
- **Oregon Ed Trail, oregonedtrail.org** (chosen): memorable pun for parents, says education; the tagline carries the
  money. Costs: reads less clearly in lowercase ("oregoned trail"), so print it as OregonEdTrail.org.
- **Oregon School Money, oregonschoolmoney.org**: clearest and cleanest in lowercase, sounds more serious to
  journalists and legislative staff. Less memorable, and those readers aren't the main audience.
- **Oregon School Funding, oregonschoolfunding.org**: most neutral, but could pass for an official state site.
- **Oregon Paper Trail**: fits the primary sources, but "paper trail" suggests digging for wrongdoing.
- **Keep Oregon K-12 Funding Research and fundoregonschools.org**: no work, but keeps the advocacy-sounding domain.

## Revisit when
Journalists or legislative staff become a main audience, or readers regularly misread or mistype the domain.
